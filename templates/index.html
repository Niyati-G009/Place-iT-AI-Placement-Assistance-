Placement FAQs

What is an ATS Score?
It measures how well your resume matches job descriptions for automated screening systems used by top companies.
How to improve CGPA?
Focus on consistent study habits and active participation in practical labs and projects.
Best Internship Time?
Summer breaks between 2nd and 3rd year are most critical for building industry experience.
Skills in Demand?
Full-stack development, AI/ML, and Cloud architecture are top priorities in 2025.
Coding Practice?
Use LeetCode or GeeksForGeeks daily to strengthen your DSA — aim for 2-3 problems per day.
What is STAR Method?
A proven technique for behavioral interview answers: Situation, Task, Action, Result.
